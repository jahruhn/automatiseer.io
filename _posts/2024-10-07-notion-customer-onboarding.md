# Automated Customer Onboarding: Your Ticket to Happy Customers

You just signed a new customer. **Awesome!** But now comes the onboarding process—creating Notion pages, assigning tasks, sending welcome emails… It’s a lot. What if you could **automate all of it**?

## How It Works (Your New Onboarding Fairy Godmother)

1. **📝 Customer Signup**: The workflow kicks off as soon as a new customer signs up. No manual triggers needed!
2. **📄 Notion Page Creation**: A dedicated Notion page is created for the customer, complete with all their details.
3. **📋 Task Assignment**: Onboarding tasks are automatically assigned to your team. No more forgotten steps!
4. **📩 Welcome Email**: A personalized welcome email is sent, making your new customer feel like a VIP.

## Why You’ll Love It
- **💖 Improve Retention**: A smooth onboarding experience = happier customers who stick around.
- **⏳ Save Time**: No more manual setup. Spend that time growing your business instead.
- **🤝 Collaboration**: All customer data is centralized in Notion, so your team stays on the same page.
